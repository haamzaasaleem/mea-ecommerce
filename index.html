Our story

Dressed for every day

MEA began with one question: why save the beautiful pieces for special occasions? We design sets you can throw on for a school run, a long lunch or a dinner that turns into a late night, without trying too hard.

Every collection is small, every fabric is chosen by hand, and every piece is finished in our own studio.
